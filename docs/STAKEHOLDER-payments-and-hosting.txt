------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. PAYMENT METHODS TODAY  (already working)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are TWO sides to payments. Please keep them separate in your mind:

  • ADMIN side  = YOU set which methods to offer, and enter the accounts
                  where money should be RECEIVED.
                  (Admin panel  ->  Settings  ->  "Payment methods")

  • CUSTOMER side = The shopper only sees the methods you switched ON, and
                    pays into the accounts you entered.
                    (Store  ->  Checkout  ->  "Payment Method")

So the payment section you saw in the Admin Settings is the ADMIN control.
Whatever you enable + fill there is what the customer sees at checkout.

WHAT WORKS TODAY (all 100% functional, no fees, no approval needed):

  1) Cash on Delivery
       - Customer pays the courier when the parcel arrives. No setup.

  2) Bank Transfer  (e.g. your Meezan Bank account)
       - You enter Bank name / Account title / Account number / IBAN in
         Settings. The customer sees those details at checkout, transfers
         the money from their own bank/app, and types the Transaction ID.

  3) JazzCash  (manual)
       - You enter your JazzCash number + account title in Settings.
         Customer sends money to it and enters the Transaction ID.

  4) EasyPaisa  (manual)
       - Same as JazzCash, with your EasyPaisa number.

HOW A MANUAL PAYMENT ORDER FLOWS:
  Customer pays -> enters Transaction ID -> order is created as "On hold"
  -> you see the Transaction ID in Admin -> Orders -> you confirm the money
  arrived in your account -> you change the order to "Processing/Paid".
  (This is the safe, standard way for a new store — you verify every payment.)

IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT THE CARD OPTION:
  The old "Debit / Credit Card (sandbox mode)" you saw was a TEST/demo only —
  it could NOT actually charge a real card. We have now HIDDEN it from the
  customer so no one is misled. Real card payments require a licensed payment
  gateway (see section 2). The switch stays in Admin Settings ("Card — needs
  setup") and we turn it on the moment a real gateway is connected.

WHY WE DON'T COLLECT CARD NUMBERS DIRECTLY:
  By law (and card-network rules called "PCI-DSS") a normal business is NOT
  allowed to take and store customers' raw card numbers on its own website.
  That is exactly what a payment gateway is for — the customer types their
  card on the gateway's secure page, and the money lands in your account.

 2. AUTOMATIC PAYMENTS (production-ready) — cards + wallets in one click
------------------------------------------------------------------------

To let customers pay by card / JazzCash / EasyPaisa INSTANTLY and
automatically (money straight to your account, order auto-confirmed), you
sign up with a licensed "payment gateway / aggregator". An AGGREGATOR is the
all-in-one option: ONE account gives you Visa/Mastercard + JazzCash +
EasyPaisa + bank — you don't chase each one separately.

WHAT YOU MUST PROVIDE TO GET APPROVED (KYC — same for all of them):
  - Registered business name (sole proprietor is fine to start)
  - NTN (tax number from FBR)
  - Business bank account (where your sales money is deposited)
  - CNIC of the owner
  - The live website + basic details
  - Approval usually takes ~3 days to ~2 weeks.

THE MAIN OPTIONS IN PAKISTAN:

  A) SAFEPAY   (getsafepay.com)   <-- RECOMMENDED for a new startup
       - Modern, easy to sign up, developer-friendly (fast for us to wire).
       - Accepts cards + wallets.
       - Setup fee: none / very low.
       - Per sale: roughly ~2.9% + a small fixed fee (confirm current rate).
       - Best balance of easy onboarding + reliability + low cost.

  B) PAYFAST   (by Bank Alfalah)   <-- best LOCAL coverage, all-in-one
       - One integration = cards + JazzCash + EasyPaisa + bank transfer.
       - Setup fee: a one-time fee may apply (confirm).
       - Per sale: roughly ~2%–3% (varies by method; confirm current rate).
       - Great if most of your customers use JazzCash/EasyPaisa.

  C) PAYMOB PAKISTAN   (paymob.com)
       - Aggregator, cards + wallets. Similar pricing. Good alternative.
       - (Our code already has a Paymob "slot" ready — quickest to switch on
         if you choose Paymob.)

  Direct JazzCash / EasyPaisa MERCHANT accounts also exist (~1.5%–2.5% per
  sale), but that's TWO separate integrations instead of one aggregator, so
  more paperwork for slightly lower fees.

MY RECOMMENDATION (cost-effective + reliable + easiest for a small startup):
  -> Start with SAFEPAY for the smoothest sign-up and clean pricing.
     If your buyers are mostly JazzCash/EasyPaisa users, choose PAYFAST
     instead for the widest local wallet coverage in one account.
  Either way it's ONE aggregator account, and once you send us the API keys
  we switch on real card/wallet checkout (usually within a day of testing).

ROUGH COST SUMMARY FOR AUTOMATIC PAYMENTS:
  - To set up:     usually FREE or a small one-time fee.
  - Per sale:      ~2%–3% of the order (this is normal worldwide).
  - Monthly:       none (you only pay a % when you actually make a sale).
  (Please confirm the exact live rates on the provider's website before
   signing — they change from time to time.)

 3. HOSTING + DOMAIN — what to buy to go fully production-ready
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The site is built as 3 parts. Here is where each should live for a real,
reliable launch, with cost-effective picks for a startup.

  DOMAIN NAME (your web address, e.g. rosynx.com)
     - Buy a ".com" — best for brand + customer trust worldwide.
       Recommended registrar: Namecheap or Cloudflare (~$10–13 / year).
     - Optional: also grab ".pk" / ".com.pk" from PKNIC (~Rs 2,000–4,000 for
       2 years) if you want the local identity too.
     -> RECOMMENDATION: buy rosynx.com (~$12/year). Cheap, one-time yearly.

  STOREFRONT + ADMIN PANEL  (the two Next.js sites)
     - Host: VERCEL (where they already run).
       * Free "Hobby" plan is fine for testing and light traffic.
       * "Pro" plan is $20/month — get it only when you have real commercial
         traffic or need a custom domain with team features.
     - Very reliable, fast, and no server maintenance for you.

  BACKEND / API + DATABASE
     - Backend host: RENDER.
       * Free plan works but "sleeps" when idle (first visit is slow ~10s).
       * Upgrade to RENDER STARTER = $7/month -> always on, no slow starts.
         This is the single most worthwhile upgrade for a live store.
     - Database: NEON (PostgreSQL).
       * Free plan is okay to launch; it "pauses" when idle.
       * Paid plan (~$19/month) when traffic grows -> always on + backups.
     - Email: BREVO — free for 300 emails/day (order + reset emails). Paid
       (~$9/month) only if you send more. No domain purchase required.
     - Product images: CLOUDINARY free tier is enough to start.

  ---------------------------------------------------------------

     Domain (Namecheap .com) ........... ~$12 / year   (~$1/month)
     Storefront + Admin (Vercel Free) .. $0

     Database (Neon Free for now) ...... $0
     Email (Brevo Free) ................ $0
     ---------------------------------------------------------
     TOTAL to go live properly:  about $7 / month + ~$12 / year domain.

  When sales grow, upgrade in this order: Neon paid ($19) -> Vercel Pro ($20).
  A comfortable "growing store" setup lands around $30–45 / month total.
